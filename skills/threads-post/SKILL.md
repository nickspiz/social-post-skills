# Threads post

Read `post-basics` first. It covers facts, the invented-claims guard, topic mix, timing and the final checklist.

## When to use

A post or reply for Threads. Write it fresh, with its own opening and shape. If you already have an X or Bluesky post about the same fact, this one must look different.

## Voice

Casual and human, the closest to how people talk. First person on a personal account, "we" on a brand account. No hashtag blocks, no engagement bait ("comment YES if..."), no sarcasm for effect.

## Form

- Limit: 500 characters. Most posts should be far shorter. A good share is one sentence. Threads also allows a free 10,000-character text attachment.
- Plain text. No Unicode "bold" letters. Emoji: at most one.
- Threads allows one topic tag per post. Skip it unless it genuinely helps, and never stack tags.
- Links: one at most, only when it is the point.
- Rotate: observation, question, short list, a small behind-the-scenes fact (from the facts list only), reaction to confirmed news.
- Neighbouring posts must not share an opening or ending.

## Images

- A brand account does not post to Threads text-only. Give it a designed picture with real product UI inside, or hold the post until there is one.
- A personal account: plain real screenshot on some posts, many posts as text. See `post-basics`, section 6.
- A carousel with up to 10 images/videos is fine when each picture carries one clear step. Add alt text.

## Topics

Mostly the product and the people making it. Competitor and trend posts at most 1 in 4-5. Questions to your audience work well here, but ask only what you would actually act on.

## Replies

Reply in conversations you were part of or where you have real experience. The platform rewards back-and-forth, so answer people who reply to you, briefly and specifically. Do not turn replies into ads.

## Example

Facts: "Pinecone Shop saved carts, shipped 3 March."

> Asked myself today why carts empty out when someone closes a tab. Fixed it: they don't anymore.

Check the facts: "asked myself today" is a claim about the author's day. Only keep it if the author said so. A safe version is "Carts in Pinecone Shop no longer empty when you close the tab."
