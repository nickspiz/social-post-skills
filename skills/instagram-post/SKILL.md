# Instagram post

Read `post-basics` first. It covers facts, the invented-claims guard, topic mix, timing and the final checklist.

## When to use

A single image or a carousel plus its caption. This skill does not cover Reels, Stories or video.

## The picture comes first

On Instagram the image is the post and the caption is the support. Decide the picture, then write the caption.

- **Brand account:** a designed picture for this post: the brand's own background and typeface, one short line of text, and real product UI inside a device or window frame. Not a raw screenshot of the website, not a redrawn imitation of the interface, not stock.
- **Personal account:** a plain, real screenshot or photo, no banner, no headline laid over it. See `post-basics`, section 6.
- Keep text on the image short: one line that can be read on a phone in two seconds. Do not leave a single word alone on its own line in a headline.
- Alt text on every image.

## Carousels

- One idea per slide. Slide 1 is the hook: the point, not a title page. The last slide is a plain takeaway, not "follow for more".
- 3-7 slides is a good range. Check the current slide maximum in Instagram Help; long carousels lose people.
- Same layout, same margins, same type size on every slide, so the swipe feels like one thing.
- Every claim on a slide follows the facts list. Do not put a number on a slide that is not in it.
- Do not mention in the caption slides that are not in the carousel.

## Caption

- Voice: warm and plain. Brand: "we". Personal: "I".
- The first line shows before "more": put the point there.
- Length: 1-4 short lines is fine. Longer only when the content is a real how-to. Meta's API documentation caps a caption at 2,200 characters; check Instagram Help for the current in-app limit.
- Emoji: one or two is normal here, not a wall.
- Hashtags: few or none. Check the current hashtag limit in Instagram Help before adding any. Never a block of 20.
- Links in captions are not clickable. Say where to find it ("link in bio") only if the bio link really leads there.

## Topics

Product features shown in use, short how-tos, before and after, the story of how something was made. Competitor or trend posts at most 1 in 4-5, confirmed sources only.

## Replies

Reply to comments on your own posts quickly and specifically. Outside your own posts, comment only where you have real experience.

## Example

Facts: "Pinecone Shop saved carts, shipped 3 March."

Slide 1: "Your cart now waits for you." (app screenshot in a phone frame)
Slide 2: "Close the tab." Slide 3: "Come back tomorrow." Slide 4: "Everything's still there."

Caption: "Saved carts are live in Pinecone Shop. Nothing to turn on."

"Nothing to turn on" is only allowed if the facts list says so.

Checked 11.10.2026: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media (caption cap of 2,200 characters for API publishing). Slide and hashtag limits for the app could not be confirmed in official docs, so no number is given.
