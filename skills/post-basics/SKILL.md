# Post basics

Rules that hold on every network. The per-network skills add form (length, formatting, images) on top of these. If a network skill and this one disagree, the network skill wins on form and this one wins on facts.

## 0. Gather before you write

Ask for these, or find them in the project. Do not start without the first two.

1. **Facts.** A short list of things that are true and checked: features that exist, dates, prices, numbers, links to the source (changelog, pricing page, official announcement, your own dashboard). Everything in a post must trace back to a line here.
2. **Voice sample.** Three to five posts the author actually wrote, or a paragraph describing the voice. Follow the sample over the defaults below.
3. **Account type.** Personal account (first person, "I") or brand account (the company speaks, "we"). A brand account never writes as the founder.
4. **What was posted lately.** The last 10 posts or the pending queue, to avoid repeating a fact, a number or a template.

## 1. What to post about

- **About 75-80% is about your own product:** real features (checked against the product itself), your own articles, the story of how it is made.
- **At most 1 post in 4-5 per week is about competitors or niche trends**, and then always with your own angle, calm, no bashing.
- Every post answers four things: who is this for, why does it matter, what can they do, how does it connect to what you make. A bare list of stats or a random piece of news fails this.
- One story per day per brand. Do not retell the same story on every network the same day.
- Do not repeat a fact, a number or a theme within about a week. Scan the whole pending queue, not only the neighbouring posts.

## 2. Facts and the invented-claims guard

This is the rule that matters most.

- **Every number, date, price, quote and claim must map to a line in the facts list.** If it does not, cut it or ask. Do not "round up" and do not guess.
- **No invented experience.** Not "I've watched this fail many times", not "people keep asking me", not "Monday is usually the slow day". Experience the author has not told you about does not exist.
- **No invented stories**, composite customers or "a user told me" unless the author gave you that exact exchange.
- **Claims about your own product** (code, pricing history, what is shared with what) are checked against the product first. Memory and assumption fail here more often than anywhere else.
- **No rumours.** Build competitor or trend posts only on a confirmed source: official announcement, documentation, pricing page, published research. A Reddit or forum thread is not a source of facts. Never open with "A thread on r/something says...".
- **Never upgrade a hedge.** "Is testing" does not become "rolled out". If the source says testing, either confirm the stronger claim from the official source or change the topic.
- **Only strong numbers.** A number that makes you look small (a handful of signups, 10 followers) is better left out. A post without a number is fine. This is about leaving weak numbers out, never about inflating or hiding context to mislead.
- **Never promise an attachment in the text** ("PDF below", "see the slides") unless it is attached at publish time, and never promise more than it contains ("lists all twenty" when it shows five).

## 3. Variety

- **Vary length.** On short-form networks some posts are literally one sentence. Most are short or medium, long ones are rare. Same length everywhere looks mechanical.
- **Vary shape:** opinion, observation, question, short list, reaction to news, before and after, a small how-to.
- **Neighbouring posts must not share a template** (same opening, same ending, same length).
- No hashtag lists, no punchline at the end, no "lol", no emoji walls unless the network skill says the network is that kind of place.

## 4. Replies and comments

- Reply only where the author has real experience, and only under live threads that are being discussed now.
- Add something: a fact, a specific, an honest answer. Do not argue and do not advertise.
- Mention the product only when it genuinely answers the question.
- Do not invent a personal experience to sound relatable.

## 5. Timing

- Weekdays only. Nothing is planned or published on Saturday and Sunday.
- One daily window, a few hours long, in the author's own time zone. Pick it once and keep everything inside it. Spread the posts inside the window, not on a round grid.
- Leave time after the window to read the reactions.

## 6. Images

- **Personal account:** a plain, real screenshot, as if the author took it. No banner, no headline on top, no caption, no background. No secrets, tokens, emails, real user data. No revenue numbers that are not already public.
- **Brand account:** a picture designed for this post, with real product UI inside a device or window frame. Not a raw screenshot of the website, not a redrawn "fake" interface, not a random stock image.
- Alt text on every image, describing what is actually shown.
- If the text depends on the image, the image must be there when the post goes out.

## 7. Final pass

Before a draft is called ready:

1. Run the human-writing pass with [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (MIT), a checklist of 35 patterns from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing". Read its section on false positives too: do not scrub real details, honest hedges or deliberate repetition.
2. No em dashes. Use a hyphen, comma, colon or two sentences.
3. No facts, numbers or quotes appeared that are not in the facts list, and none from the list got lost.
4. Check the checklist below.

## Checklist

- [ ] Account type is right: "I" on personal, "we" on brand.
- [ ] Topic fits the 75-80% / 1-in-4-or-5 mix; competitor posts have our angle and a confirmed source.
- [ ] Every number, date and claim maps to the facts list.
- [ ] No invented experience, no invented story.
- [ ] No rumour, no "thread on r/X" opening, no hedge upgraded.
- [ ] Says who it is for, why it matters, what to do.
- [ ] Not a repeat of a fact or theme from the last week; not the same template as its neighbours.
- [ ] Length differs from the previous posts.
- [ ] No promise about an attachment that is not there.
- [ ] Image follows the account type; alt text written.
- [ ] Weekday, inside the daily window.
- [ ] Human-writing pass done, no em dashes.
