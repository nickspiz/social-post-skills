# X post

Read `post-basics` first. It covers facts, the invented-claims guard, topic mix, timing and the final checklist.

## When to use

A post, a reply or a short thread for X. If the same idea also goes to Bluesky, write the Bluesky version separately with `bluesky-post`. Do not copy.

## Voice

Human and plain. A person talking, not a campaign. Personal account in first person, brand account as "we". No sarcasm for effect, no "lol", no hashtags, no ending with a punchline.

## Form

- Length: The standard limit is 280 characters per post. X Premium subscribers can write longer posts, up to 25,000 characters, on every Premium tier. Aim well under the limit. Part of the posts should be one sentence only. Medium posts are fine, long ones are rare. Threads only when the content really needs steps.
- **No Unicode "bold" or "italic" letters.** They break search and screen readers. Make emphasis with structure: line breaks, a short list, one strong sentence on its own line.
- Emoji: at most one per post, often none.
- Hashtags: none.
- Links: they cost reach on some days and are fine on others. Use one only when the link is the point.
- Rotate formats: opinion, observation, a question, a short list, a reaction to confirmed news, a small tip. Neighbouring posts must not share an opening or an ending.

## Images

- Up to 4 images per post.
- Images are not required. Alternate: one post with an image, the next without.
- Personal account: plain real screenshot. Brand account: a designed image with real product UI. See `post-basics`, section 6.
- Always add alt text.

## Topics

Mostly your own product and what you learned making it. Trend or competitor posts at most 1 in 4-5 and only from confirmed sources. Do not build a post on a screenshot of someone else's complaint.

## Replies

On X replies are often where the reach is. Rules:

- Under a thread that is being discussed right now, in your niche, where you have real experience.
- One specific useful sentence beats three polite ones.
- Do not reply with your product link. Do not argue with strangers.
- If you have no honest experience of the topic, do not reply.

## Threads (multi-post)

Post 1 must stand alone and carry the point. Number nothing unless there are real steps. Do not end with "follow for more".

## Example

Facts: "Pinecone Shop now has saved carts (changelog, 3 March)."

> Saved carts are live in Pinecone Shop. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, the cart is still there.

A one-sentence post like this is normal. Do not pad it.
